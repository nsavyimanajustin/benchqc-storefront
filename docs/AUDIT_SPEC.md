## Quality Grade Thresholds

| Grade | Overall Score | Battery Wear | Minimum Student Battery Runtime | Thermal Throttling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade A+** | $\ge 95$ | $\ge 90\%$ Health | $\ge 4.5$ Hours (Medium Load) | 0% |
| **Grade A** | $85 - 94$ | $\ge 80\%$ Health | $\ge 3.5$ Hours (Medium Load) | $< 5\%$ |
| **Grade B** | $70 - 84$ | $\ge 65\%$ Health | $\ge 2.5$ Hours (Medium Load) | $< 15\%$ |
| **Fail / Recondition** | $< 70$ | $< 65\%$ Health | $< 2.5$ Hours | Heavy Throttling |
